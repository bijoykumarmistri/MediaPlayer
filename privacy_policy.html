Privacy Policy for BKM Player

Last Updated: November 2026

BKM Player ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you use BKM Player.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

BKM Player is designed as an offline-first local media player. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information, usage analytics, or identity data to external servers or third parties.

2. Permissions and Their Usage

  • Photos and Videos (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used exclusively to index, display, and play video files stored locally on your device.
  • Audio and Music (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used exclusively to scan, display, and play audio files and music tracks stored locally on your device.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Used to keep audio and video playback playing continuously when the screen is turned off or when you navigate to other apps. A notification with playback controls is displayed while active.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to display media playback controls (play, pause, skip, album art) in your notification drawer and lock screen.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely if you stream an online media URL or check for official updates. The app does not track your network identity.
  • Modify Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used to adjust volume and handle audio focus changes (such as pausing during incoming phone calls).

3. Privacy Vault (Hidden Media)

BKM Player includes an optional local Privacy Vault feature:

  • Media moved to the Vault is stored securely in your device's private application storage.
  • Your PIN and security answers are hashed using cryptographically secure algorithms (salted SHA-256) and kept locally on your device.
  • We have no access to your PIN, security questions, or vaulted media files.

4. Children's Privacy

BKM Player does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The app is a standalone local media player with no user accounts or social interactions.
